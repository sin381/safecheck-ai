# safecheck-ai
“AI-powered fake profile risk checker for women’s safety”
SafeCheck AI 

AI-Assisted Online Safety for Women

1. About the Project

SafeCheck AI is a prototype designed to help women identify potentially suspicious online profiles and messages. It aims to promote safer online interactions by highlighting warning signs and providing practical safety guidance.

2. Problem Statement

Online harassment, fake profiles, impersonation, scams, and suspicious messages can make online platforms less safe. Users may find it difficult to recognize warning signs or know what steps to take.

SafeCheck AI aims to make these warning signs easier to understand and help users take informed action.

3. Proposed Solution

SafeCheck AI is designed to help users assess potentially suspicious online activity and access relevant safety resources.

Key Features

- Profile Assessment: Enter profile details for a preliminary safety assessment.
- Suspicious Message Analysis: Check pasted messages for potential warning signs.
- Risk Levels: Display Low, Medium, or High risk levels based on implemented checks.
- Explainable Results: Explain the warning signs identified by the system.
- Screenshot Preview: Preview an uploaded screenshot in the browser, if implemented.
- Safety Guidance: Provide relevant reporting instructions and safety resources.
- Report Draft: Help users prepare a report using information they provide, if implemented.

7. Disclaimer

SafeCheck AI provides a preliminary assessment, not proof that an account or message is fraudulent or safe. Results may be incomplete or inaccurate. Users should exercise caution and verify information independently.
